### Summary

The mockups are structurally strong and unusually well-traced — the screen set matches the confirmed eight, the actor-trust rule and the shared-counting guarantee are respected consistently across both documents, and the accessibility commitments are specific enough to become testable requirements. What holds it back is arithmetic and diagram work rather than product judgement: the dashboard's headline numbers violate the invariant the documents themselves state (R-01), and the transition diagram draws the recovery edge to the wrong destination and omits two edges from the table it is captioned with (R-02). Both are defects a developer would build from directly, and both are cheap to fix; the four Major findings are the same class of thing — an assertable inconsistency between what one part of the pair says and what another part says — and all four are localised edits. A reviewer approving on the strength of the prose alone would be approving numbers and arrows that do not hold.
