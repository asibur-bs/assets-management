### Summary

The set is strong on the decisions that matter most — the shared counting function is treated as one indivisible group, the reason-bearing dialogs explain their consequence before asking, the actor indicator is honestly marked provisional, and the deferrals are recorded with reasons rather than silently dropped. Two Critical findings block engineering: the approved `in-stock → lost` transition is absent from both transition tables, and the F5a transition map as drawn authorises re-assigning and re-losing a discarded asset, contradicting its own F5d prose and the W3 actions table. Three Major findings follow — the holder-directory chip arithmetic does not reconcile with its own expanded rows, CSV export has no flow and no formula-injection story despite being a named release must-have, and the W2 Holder column's semantics are undefined.
