### Summary

The three findings raised in the previous round are genuinely resolved, and I verified each one independently rather than accepting the stated fix. R-01 is fully fixed: I re-derived the unit dependency edges from scratch by applying the artifact's own stated rule to `components.md`'s `Depends On` column after mapping all twenty-one components to units, and the ten edges the rule produces match the yaml block, the prose edge table, and the ASCII adjacency block exactly, with none missing and none extra. The block is structurally sound — six units named once each, every `depends_on` resolving, no self-dependency, acyclic by traversal, all `kind:` values inside the enum and matching the Unit Table's Kind column — and the "complete set" claim about parallel opportunities is TRUE against it. R-02 and R-03 are fixed as described in the tool table. All six sensors pass.

The deliverables are also sound on everything else I was asked to check: the twenty-one components are each assigned to exactly one unit and no ADR that keeps two components apart is violated by the grouping; every one of the thirty released stories is assigned to a unit among the units of the components the domain design bound it to; all ten deferred items carry attachment points matching the domain design's own binding; the per-unit counts agree across the Unit Table, a recount of the Story Assignment Table, and `traceability.json`; both team-mandated indivisible groups hold; and the stage's forbidden content is absent — no implementation order between units, no critical path, no "ship this first", and no actor-identity mechanism candidate named.

One defect stands unresolved, and it is why the verdict is NOT-READY. The Complexity Bands section promises that each band is a disjunction, that each unit "names the clause it qualifies on", and that the band is therefore "auditable rather than asserted". U5 owns seven components, which satisfies the XL band's first disjunct ("More than six components") outright, but the artifact's rebuttal addresses only XL's second disjunct — the guarantee clause — and leaves the first one unanswered. Under the rule the section itself states, U5 qualifies for XL, and its L rating inverts the relative ordering against U3, which the band exists to express. R-02 is the same class of defect in a milder form: U6's sizing rests entirely on the "new configuration" clause of M, and unlike the other five units it does not say so. R-03 is not a defect in this stage's deliverables — the eight-code reading they take is the one ADR-012 and the binding table support — but the upstream catalogue's responsibility list says "nine", and that count should be corrected in the domain-design artifact so the two readings are not left live.

Nothing here touches the dependency DAG, the story map, the traceability contract, or the stage's forbidden-content rules; the graph, the mapping, and the counts are correct and independently verified. The fix is confined to one section of one file, plus one upstream correction. I expect the next round to pass once the XL clause is resolved against U5's component count.
