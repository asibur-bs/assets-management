### Assessment

I checked the stage's own declared review focus by hand against the story set's named eight-asset reconciliation seed: the derived-counts table (Total Records 8, Active Inventory 7, buckets 2/2/1/2/1, available-to-assign 2, directory 2 holders / 3 rows, Dana 4, Chris 1, Unassigned 3 with chips [In stock 2][Lost 1], 8 CSV rows) is entirely correct, the eight released edges reconcile exactly against the W3 action table, and the three loss-dialog variants, the four count-consuming surfaces and the 30-story trace all check out. The wireframes correction the stage carries forward is legitimate and correctly reasoned, and the stylesheet mapping was verified line by line against `public/styles.css`. However, the drawn frames do not hold the arithmetic the prose states: W2's table breaks down as 2/1/3/2/0 against the seed's 2/2/1/2/1 and omits the discarded asset that W6 attributes to Chris Patel, W3 populates a `Last holder` on a currently-held asset whose seed previous holder is null (against AC5.1.9 and against the document's own sentence two hundred lines earlier), and the count line — the release's central consistency guarantee — renders the matching figure and a total rather than the shown and matching figures FR6.4 requires, while the directory count line never renders the qualifier that AC5.3.8 and the accessibility checklist both say it carries. These are silent failures of exactly the class the stage asked the reviewer to concentrate on: none raises an error, and R-1 and R-2 in particular would carry a false claim about the release's own counting and holder definitions into the next stage, which is why the verdict is NOT-READY.
