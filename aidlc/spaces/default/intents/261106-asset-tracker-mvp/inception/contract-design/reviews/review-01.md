### Summary

The contract set is well-built on the parts that carry the release's central guarantees: the eight transition edges, the seven audit actions, the eight error codes, the actor-trust rule, the CSV escaping rule and the migration ordering are all bound and consistent with their upstream artifacts, and no contract introduces a dependency the DAG forbids. Three Major findings nevertheless block approval: the audit-history read that the units-generation seam names as crossing U3→U5 has no contract at all (R-01); the additive-change rule asserts that adding a value to a vocabulary is always safe, which directly contradicts the contract set's own hard bindings of exactly five statuses and exactly eight transition edges and would let a sixth status or a ninth edge enter the release (R-02); and contract 7 assigns the filter-consuming read to U3's `AssetRepository` while contract 9 separately gives U4's `AssetQuery` the same operation, leaving the single-query-construction mechanism that INV-3 rests on unstated and open to drift (R-03). The five Minor findings are each cheap to correct and are recorded for the same pass.
