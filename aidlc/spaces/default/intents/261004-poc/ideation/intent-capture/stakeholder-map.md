# Stakeholder Map

This is a `poc`-scope map. It captures the directly identified stakeholders for this proof of concept; broader organizational stakeholders (security, legal, procurement) are deferred to a later scope once the core idea is validated.

## Stakeholders

| Stakeholder | Role | Interest | Authority | Source |
|-------------|------|----------|-----------|--------|
| The developer (you) | Builder, operator, and primary decision-maker | A working proof that the core idea holds; an opportunity to learn the chosen stack end-to-end without a heavy process overhead | Sole decision-maker for scope and priority within this `poc` | [Q6] [Q7] |
| Internal employees (IT, HR, Finance, Operations) | Primary users of the eventual system | A tool that lets them register, locate, and reconcile assets in a small number of clicks, replacing today's spreadsheets / email / paper | Influence through feedback once the proof is usable; not decision-makers in the `poc` | [Q2] [Q3] [Q4] |

## Decision-Makers vs. Influencers

- **Decision-maker**: The developer (you). You set scope, priority, and the cut-line for what is in the `poc` versus deferred. [Q7]
- **Influencers**: The internal employees who will eventually use the system. Their feedback shapes what is "usable enough" to declare the proof successful, but they do not gate scope decisions in the `poc` stage. [Q2] [Q6]

## Communication Requirements

- No formal reporting cadence is required for this `poc`. Communication is self-directed — progress and decisions are tracked through the workflow artifacts and the approval gates built into each stage. [Q8]
